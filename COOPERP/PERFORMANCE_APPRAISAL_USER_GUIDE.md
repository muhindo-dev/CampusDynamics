# Performance Appraisal System — User Guide

**Muteesa I Royal University · Human Resource Management**
*Last updated: October 2026*

> **What changed in October 2026.** Section B now follows the Council's *Evaluation Form for Achievements of Staff
> Responsibilities and Key Performance Areas*: for each responsibility you record your **achievement** against the
> **expected standard** for your office, with **evidence** (files can be attached). Appraisals are signed
> electronically, and the employee acknowledges the supervisor's rating at the end. The portal reminds staff
> when an appraisal is waiting.
>
> The appraisal is **not** the contract renewal application. A completed appraisal is one of the documents a
> renewal application needs; contract renewal will be a separate module.

## The process from A to Z

| # | Step | Who | Where | Status afterwards |
|---|------|-----|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Open the session (records are created for all staff, each with a reviewer) | HR | eadmin → Appraisal Sessions → Activate | `PENDING` |
| 2 | Fill in the self appraisal, attach evidence, sign and submit | Employee | Staff Portal → My Appraisals | `EMPLOYEE_IN_PROGRESS` → `EMPLOYEE_SUBMITTED` |
| 3 | Review, rate, sign — or return for revision | Supervisor | Staff Portal → My Appraisals → Review queue | `SUPERVISOR_IN_PROGRESS` → `COMPLETED` (or `RETURNED`) |
| 4 | Read the result and acknowledge it (agree / disagree) | Employee | Staff Portal (popup / home page / My Appraisals) | `COMPLETED` (acknowledged) |
| 5 | HR review and recommendation | HR | eadmin → Appraisal View | `HR_REVIEWED` |
| 6 | Reports and print | HR / Management | eadmin → Reports, Session Report, Print | — |

Who the reviewer is: the supervisor set on the staff profile; if none, the head of the employee's department; if
none, the head of the department on the employee's current contract. Staff without any of these see a warning
and can set their supervisor themselves (**Set my supervisor**); HR can also assign reviewers in bulk
(eadmin → Appraisal View → filter *No reviewer assigned* → *Assign Reviewer*). An appraisal cannot be submitted
without a reviewer.

## Reminders in the portal

When a staff member opens the portal while something is waiting, a popup appears (close it with **×**, **Esc** or
**Remind me later**; it comes back at the next login or when the situation changes). The same message stays as an
alert card on the home page until the action is done. It shows:

- **Start** — the appraisal is open and not yet started;
- **Continue** — how far the draft has got (progress ring);
- **Revise & resubmit** — the supervisor returned it, with their reason;
- **Read & acknowledge** — the supervisor has completed the review;
- for supervisors, how many staff appraisals are waiting for their review;
- the days left to the session deadline (amber within 7 days, red when passed).

## Stage 2 — Self appraisal (employee)

1. Portal → **My Appraisals** → **Start / Continue**.
2. **Setup** — tick the four confirmations: supervisor, profile, staff category, and the set of expected
   standards for your office (change it if it is not your office).
3. **Section A** — your details (read-only).
4. **Section B — Achievements of Responsibilities & Key Performance Areas.** The expected standards for your office
   are listed. For each one:
   - **Achievement** — what you achieved during the period;
   - **Evidence** — what proves it (course outlines, mark sheets, minutes, reports, publications, certificates…);
     you may **attach files** (PDF, image, Word, Excel; up to 5 MB, 5 files per item);
   - **Self-rating** 1–5, or **N/A** with a reason if the standard does not apply to your role.
   Add your other responsibilities with **+ Add another responsibility**. Support staff (no Council list) enter
   their own responsibilities.
5. **Section C** — rate yourself on every competency (or N/A).
6. **Section D** — training and development needs.
7. **Section E** — answer every question, tick the **declaration** (your electronic signature) and **Submit**.

The form saves as you move between steps. Submission is refused, with the step to fix, if a responsibility is
incomplete, a competency is unrated, a question is blank, the declaration is not ticked, or no reviewer is set.
After submission the form is locked unless the supervisor returns it.

## Stage 3 — Supervisor review

1. Portal → **My Appraisals** → **Review queue** → **Review**.
2. Section B: read each achievement and its evidence (open the attached files), give your rating 1–5 and a
   comment where your rating differs. You have the final say on N/A: rating an item the employee marked N/A brings
   it back into the score; choosing N/A takes it out.
3. Section C: rate every competency (or N/A). Section D: adjust the training plan.
4. **Score Summary** → tick the reviewer declaration → **Complete Review**. Or **Return** with a reason.

The employee is emailed and asked to acknowledge. Only HR can reopen a completed review.

## Stage 4 — Acknowledgement (employee)

Open the appraisal (popup, home page or My Appraisals → **View & Acknowledge**), read the score and the
supervisor's ratings and comments, choose **I agree** or **I disagree** (a comment is required when disagreeing),
and submit. The printed form shows the acknowledgement.

## Stage 5 — HR review

eadmin → **Appraisal View** → open a `COMPLETED` record → HR overall rating, recommendation
(CONFIRM / EXTEND_PROBATION / PIP / PROMOTE / OTHER) and comments → `HR_REVIEWED`. HR can also return, reopen,
cancel and reassign; every action is recorded in the audit trail. HR access: users with the *admin* or
*hr_manager* role, or the Administrator / Human Resource roles.

HR maintains the expected standards in eadmin → **Expected Standards** (groups, standards and department
mapping) and competencies in **Competency Templates**.

## Scoring

Score = (supervisor ratings in Section B + supervisor ratings in Section C) ÷ (5 × number of applicable items) × 100.
Every applicable item counts — an unrated item lowers the score rather than disappearing. N/A items are excluded.

| Classification | Score |
|---|---|
| Exceptional | 90 – 100 % |
| Above Expectations | 75 – 89 % |
| Satisfactory | 60 – 74 % |
| Development Needed | 50 – 59 % |
| Unsatisfactory | below 50 % |

Item rating labels — Academic: 5 Exceptional, 4 Above Expectations, 3 Satisfactory, 2 Development Needed,
1 Unsatisfactory. Administrative and Support: 5 Excellent, 4 Very Good, 3 Good, 2 Fair, 1 Poor.

## Printing

The printed form follows the Council layout: Name, Employee No, Date of Review, Position Held, Department,
Reports To, Reviewer's Name and Title, Date Submitted; the responsibilities table (Expected Standards,
Achievements, Evidence); competencies; training plan; comments and approval; electronic signatures with space
for wet signatures. Employees can print once the supervisor has completed the review; HR and reviewers can print
at any time.

## Frequently asked questions

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I submitted by mistake. | Ask your supervisor to **Return** it, or HR. |
| My appraisal does not appear. | You may not be in the session; contact HR with your staff number. |
| It says no supervisor is assigned. | Use **Set my supervisor** on the form, or ask HR. |
| A standard does not apply to me. | Choose **N/A** and give the reason; your supervisor decides. |
| Is this my contract renewal application? | No. The completed appraisal is one of the documents the renewal application needs. |
